# Social brand assets

These original SVG files identify links to the respective services. The logos
remain the trademarks and property of their respective owners. They do not imply
endorsement of this project. Files are preserved as downloaded, without redrawing
or changing their colors; CSS controls only the display size.

- X: `logo.svg` from the [official logo archive](https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-logo.zip), linked by the [brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit).
- GitHub: `GitHub_Invertocat_White.svg` from the [official logo archive](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip), provided by the [brand toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo).
- Discord: `Discord-Symbol-White.svg` from the [official symbol archive](https://cdn.discordapp.com/assets/content/80af2c38f13b4a7d2cb3572e1220f6e958d3c3aedccc7c7d3ddc9832f6b3d725.zip), linked by the [branding page](https://discord.com/branding).
- WhatsApp: the [official SVG favicon](https://static.whatsapp.net/rsrc.php/y1/r/FJbTMJqMap7.svg) referenced by the [WhatsApp website](https://www.whatsapp.com/); see its [brand guidelines](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/).

Retrieved 2026-10-23. No third-party icon font or image-generation service is used.
